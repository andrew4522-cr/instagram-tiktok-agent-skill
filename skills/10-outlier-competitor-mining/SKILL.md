# Skill 10: Outlier Competitor Mining & Structural Cloning

Never copy a viral video's *topic*—copy its **structure**.

An **Outlier Video** is a Reel or TikTok that achieved **3x to 10x** the creator's median view count (for example, a 5,000-follower account suddenly hitting 150,000 views). That proves the **hook and pacing structure** carried the video, not the creator's celebrity status.

---

## The 3-Step Structural Extraction Method

1. **Tag the Outlier's First 3 Seconds (`Said` + `Seen` + `Overlay`):**
   - Write down the exact sentence structure, camera framing, and on-screen text used in `0:00 - 0:03`.
2. **Abstract the Skeleton (Cross-Industry Transfer):**
   - Example Outlier (Coffee Shop): *"Stop buying €6 iced lattes when you can make this in 40 seconds."*
   - Extracted Skeleton: `"Stop paying [High Price] for [Common Thing] when you can get [Better Result] in [Seconds]."`
   - Cloned for B2B / SaaS / Consulting: *"Stop paying €3,000 a month for lead lists when you can run this workflow in 40 seconds."*
3. **Keep the Exact Beat Timing:**
   - If the outlier video cut to a close-up proof shot at `0:03`, a 3-step breakdown at `0:07`, and a payoff reveal at `0:19`, mirror those exact timestamp beats with your own content.
