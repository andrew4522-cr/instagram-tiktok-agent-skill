# Skill 03: Short-Form Scriptwriter (Second-by-Second Beat Architecture)

Never write a Reel or TikTok script as a wall of paragraphs. Short-form video is a **3-track timeline** where visual changes must happen every **3 to 5 seconds** to reset viewer attention.

---

## Required Script Output Template

For every script, output this exact 4-part beat sheet:

### 1. Metadata & Setup
- **Hook Formula Used:** (From Skill 01)
- **Target Duration:** `25–32 seconds` (65–85 spoken words at 150 WPM)
- **Cover Headline (4 Words Max):** `[Text]`

### 2. Second-by-Second Beat Table

| Time Window | Visual / Camera / B-Roll (`SEEN`) | Spoken Audio (`SAID`) | On-Screen Text (`OVERLAY`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`0:00 - 0:03` (Hook)** | Fast zoom-in + physical proof shown immediately | 1 punchy sentence (8-14 words) | **3-5 word high-contrast banner** |
| **`0:03 - 0:08` (Agitation)** | Cut to angle 2 or B-roll showing the old painful way | Explain why the common approach fails | Short pain label |
| **`0:08 - 0:15` (The Shift)** | Swipe transition to the solution / live demo | Introduce the core mechanism simply | Step 1 -> Step 2 |
| **`0:15 - 0:23` (The Proof)** | Close-up on the before/after result or metric | Share the specific outcome (time/money saved) | **Exact result highlighted** |
| **`0:23 - 0:28` (Seamless CTA)** | Direct eye contact or final result on screen | 1-sentence CTA that bridges back to `0:00` | **Comment [KEYWORD] / Link in bio** |
