# Skill 09: Faceless Channel & AI B-Roll Studio

Perfect for side-hustlers, developers, writers, and busy founders who want to turn blog posts, software tools, or expertise into viral Reels and TikToks **without ever setting up a ring light or showing their face on camera**.

---

## The 4 Best-Performing Faceless Formats

1. **The "Over-the-Shoulder" Screen Choreography (`Software / AI / Finance / Tutorials`):**
   - Record your screen at `1080x1920` (or zoom in `200%` on the active cursor area).
   - Highlight the exact number/button in yellow, show the live before-and-after execution in real time, and narrate with a crisp voiceover (or ElevenLabs clone).
2. **Hands-Only POV / Tactile Workspace (`Physical Products / Cafes / Craft / Notes`):**
   - Mount the phone looking straight down at your desk, notebook, iPad, or product. Draw the diagram or demonstrate the product live with your hands while speaking.
3. **Split-Screen Proof + Kinetic Typography:**
   - Top 50% of screen: Live screen recording, chart, or visual receipt.
   - Bottom 50% of screen: Satisfying kinetic workflow diagram or tactile B-roll with bold word-by-word captions centered on the split line.
4. **AI Cinema B-Roll Prompts (`Veo / Runway / Kling`):**
   - Generates exact 5-second visual prompts with camera movement (`macro lens, slow push-in, shallow depth of field, natural morning light`) to illustrate each sentence of your voiceover.
