# Skill 07: Carousel & TikTok Photo-Mode Architect

On both Instagram and TikTok (Photo Mode), carousels generate **2x to 4x more Saves and Shares** than video when structured for thumb-swiping momentum.

---

## The Slide-1 + Slide-2 Double-Hook Rule

Instagram's algorithm automatically shows your carousel a **second time** to followers who scrolled past Slide 1—starting on **Slide 2**.
- **Slide 1 (Primary Hook):** Bold 6-word promise + visual receipt (`"How We Cut Reporting From 3 Days to 15 Mins"`).
- **Slide 2 (Re-Serve Hook + Context):** Must work as a standalone second hook (`"90% of teams waste 12 hours a week on copy-paste. Here is the 4-step fix ->"`).
- **Slides 3 to 8 (One Point Per Slide):** Maximum 25 words per slide. End every slide's bottom-right corner with a micro-cliffhanger (`"But Step 3 is where the magic happens ->"`).
- **Slide 9 (The Summary Receipt):** Put all steps on one "cheat-sheet" slide designed specifically to trigger the **Save** button.
- **Slide 10 (Action CTA):** `"Save this for your next sprint, or comment 'GUIDE' and I'll DM you the template."`
