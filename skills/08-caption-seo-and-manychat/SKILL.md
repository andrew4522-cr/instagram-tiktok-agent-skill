# Skill 08: Caption Search-SEO & Comment-to-DM Engine

In 2026, TikTok and Instagram index your **spoken transcript, on-screen text, and first 3 caption lines** for search queries just like Google. Meanwhile, "Link in bio" converts poorly compared to **Comment-to-DM automation**.

---

## The 4-Part High-Converting Caption Structure

1. **Line 1 (The Above-the-Fold Hook):**
   - Must stand alone before the `...more` truncation (under 110 characters) and include your primary search keyword naturally.
2. **Lines 2–5 (The Value Skim / Bullet Receipt):**
   - 3 short bullet points summarizing the transformation or checklist so readers stay on the caption for `8+ seconds` (while your video loops in the background, doubling watch time!).
3. **Line 6 (The Comment-to-DM Trigger):**
   - Instead of *"Link in bio"*, use a single-word comment trigger:
     `Comment "SYSTEM" and I will DM you the free checklist directly.`
   - Why this works: Every comment signals high engagement to the Reels/TikTok algorithm AND opens a warm DM conversation automatically.
4. **Bottom Line (5 Hyper-Specific Search Hashtags):**
   - Never use 30 generic tags like `#fyp #viral`. Use exactly **5 niche search tags** (`#WorkflowAutomation`, `#B2BGrowth`, `#AIForBusiness`, etc.) so the algorithm categorizes your audience accurately.
