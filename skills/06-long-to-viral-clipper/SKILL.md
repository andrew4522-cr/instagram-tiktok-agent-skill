# Skill 06: Long-to-Viral Clipper & Repurposing Engine

Why do 95% of clipped podcast/YouTube Reels flop? Because editors just slice a 45-second chunk out of the middle of a conversation where the speaker starts with *"And another thing I noticed is..."*

Cold scrollers on TikTok and Reels have zero context. To make a repurposed clip go viral, you must **extract the high-tension core and bolt on a brand-new 0–3s cold-open hook**.

---

## The 4-Step Clipping Protocol

1. **Scan for High-Tension Moments:**
   - Look for contrarian statements, specific numbers/receipts, emotional stories, or step-by-step lists inside the long transcript or blog post.
2. **Isolate a Single Idea (`25–45 Seconds`):**
   - Never cram 3 topics into one short clip. One clip = one problem + one payoff.
3. **Engineer the "Franken-Hook" (`0:00 - 0:03`):**
   - Pull the single most shocking 6-word sentence from the *end* of the clip and place it at `0:00` as a teaser, OR record/overlay a 3-second context hook before the clip starts.
4. **Add Dynamic Subtitles & Split-Screen Proof:**
   - Pair talking-head audio with top-half B-roll or screen-recorded proof so the viewer isn't staring at a static podcast microphone for 40 seconds.
