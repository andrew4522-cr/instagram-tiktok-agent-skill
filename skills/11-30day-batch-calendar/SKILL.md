# Skill 11: 30-Day Batch Calendar & 90-Minute Filming System

Working professionals and founders quit Instagram and TikTok because trying to think of an idea, script it, film it, and edit it *every single day after work* is exhausting.

This skill generates a **30-Day Content Calendar** grouped by **Filming Setup** instead of chronological days—so you can record the entire month in one 90-minute session.

---

## The 4-Pillar Monthly Ratio

| Content Pillar | Share of Month | Goal & Metric Tracked |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Pain Callouts & Contrarian Hooks** | **35% (10 posts)** | Reach cold non-followers on FYP/Reels (`Views & 3s Hold`) |
| **2. Step-by-Step Playbooks & Carousels** | **30% (9 posts)** | Build authority & bookmark value (`Saves & Shares`) |
| **3. Before/After Proof & Case Studies** | **20% (6 posts)** | Build buyer trust (`Profile Visits & DMs`) |
| **4. Direct Offer / Comment-to-DM Triggers** | **15% (5 posts)** | Convert warm viewers into leads (`Comment Keywords & Clicks`) |

---

## The 90-Minute Batch Production Grouping

When generating the 30-day plan, group all 30 posts into **3 Batch Blocks**:
- **Batch Block A (10 Faceless / Screen-Recording Reels — 30 mins):** Film all 10 computer-screen or voiceover demos back-to-back in one sitting.
- **Batch Block B (10 Talking-Head / Desk POV Reels — 40 mins):** Film all 10 spoken scripts in one lighting setup (swap a jacket or t-shirt halfway through for visual variety).
- **Batch Block C (10 Swipe-Stop Carousels / Photo-Mode Decks — 20 mins):** Export all 10 slide decks from a single reusable template.
